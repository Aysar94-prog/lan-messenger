# Deferred ideas

- **I2-1 Encrypted Local Drafts** — Encrypt unfinished and finalized voice-message drafts in application-private storage with a per-install protected key, while preserving the planned atomic recovery registry and transactional Send sequence.
- **I2-5 Media Diagnostics Bundle** — Add privacy-safe local diagnostics for capture initialization, device changes, frame discontinuities, draft transitions, validation failures, scheduler admissions, and playback termination, with an explicit user-controlled export that excludes audio content and message text.
